The archive

200+ cartoons. Most of them still painfully relevant.

The original Wanaki ran through the dot-com boom and bust, the open-plan office revolution, and the first wave of enterprise software bloat. The archive lives here. New cartoons publish weekly on LinkedIn and Instagram.

The territory

Offices. Tech. The meeting that could've been an email.

Corporate culture, AI hype, quarterly reviews, developer theology, management speak, and the eternal gap between the slide deck and reality. Occasionally: football.

The format

Four panels. One guanaco. No moral.

The strip follows a cast of recognizable office archetypes. The guanaco closes each strip from a safe distance — observing, unimpressed, technically correct. He never explains the joke. Neither do we.
